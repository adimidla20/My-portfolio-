Exam Scanner

Planned
📊 The Problem: Engineering exam papers contain complex diagrams, mathematical equations, and multi-format questions that are difficult to digitize and organize for exam preparation. Students waste 2-3 hours manually sorting and categorizing questions.
💡 My Solution: An AI-powered scanner that extracts text, recognizes diagrams, and intelligently organizes exam papers into searchable, study-friendly formats with auto-categorization by topic, difficulty, and marks.
🎯 Key Features:
  • OCR for handwritten and printed text extraction
  • Diagram recognition and categorization
  • Auto-tagging by subject and difficulty level
  • Export to markdown or PDF with clean formatting
  • Smart search across all past papers
Python OpenCV Tesseract OCR FastAPI AI APIs

Hostel Menu Planner

Planned
📊 The Problem: Hostel mess menus are unpredictable, and students often miss their favorite meals or don't know what's being served until they arrive. No centralized system for menu tracking or notifications.
💡 My Solution: A smart menu tracker that scrapes hostel mess schedules, sends notifications for favorite dishes, and helps students plan their meals efficiently with nutritional information and weekly planning.
🎯 Key Features:
  • Daily menu notifications via Telegram/WhatsApp
  • Personal preference tracking ("notify me when paneer is served")
  • Nutritional information lookup and calorie tracking
  • Weekly meal planner and shopping list generator
  • Menu history and favorite meals tracking
